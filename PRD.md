# Project Idea: Student Academic Assistance Platform 

## 1.Proposed name: StudyConnect 

The Problem: 

        Babcock University students sometimes find some particular courses or topics difficult to understand. Meanwhile, there are other students who understand those courses or topics and are willing to help but students don't always know how to find one another. Questions posted in large group chats may be missed, and some students may feel uncomfortable asking for help publicly. 

 

## 2.Interview Questions  

1. Have you ever struggled to understand a particular course or topic? 

2. What do you normally do when you don't understand something taught in class? 

3. Have you ever struggled to find someone who could explain a difficult topic? 

4. Have you used WhatsApp groups or another platform to ask for academic help? What worked or did not work? 

5. Would you be comfortable receiving academic help from another student? Why or why not? 

  

## 3.The people who are facing the problem (Our Target Audience) 

There are different groups of people who are our target audience for this platform. The groups are: 

1. Students struggling academically: 

        These are students who have difficulty understanding certain courses or topics. 

2. Students who need further explanations: 

      These are students who may not fully understand lectures and need someone to explain differently. 

3. Students who cannot find study partners: 

      These are students who need study partners or struggle to identify classmates or other students who understand particular courses. 

4. Students willing to help others: 

     These are students who may be willing to share their knowledge but lack a platform to connect with students who need their assistance. 

PRIMARY USERS: Students seeking academic assistance  

SECONDARY USERS: Students willing to provide academic assistance. 

  

## 4.The ways by which they cope today and how they fall short. 

1. YouTube tutorials: 

     This falls short because the videos may not address the exact question a student has, and students cannot always ask the creator for immediate clarification. 

2. Asking senior students: 

   Senior students may have busy schedules or clashing classes; students may not know which senior students are knowledgeable in subjects and finding someone willing and available to help can be difficult. All these factors are the reasons why another solution must be created.  

3. Using AI like ChatGPT, Gemini and many more. 

      AI is not always correct. During the interview conducted with students, some students testified that there were times when they used AI to do their assignments and they got it wrong and sometimes AI can make things more complicated than it already is and you end up leaving more confused than you were. 

4.Whatsapp groups: 

     Academic questions can get buried under unrelated messages; students may not receive responses, and large groups may discourage some students from asking or answering questions. 

5. Re-reading lecture notes, slides and other lecture materials: 

      Students who tend to do this get frustrated and eventually give up; they end up cramming concepts for exam purposes and not understanding it. 

 

## 5.Proposed solution: 

    StudyConnect is a platform that would connect students seeking academic assistance with students that are willing to help with courses or topics they understand. The platform would organize students by courses and provide a way for students to contact each other. 

 

## 6.Product goals; 

1.Easy academic assistance: 

   Makes it easier for students to access academic assistance. 

2.Encourage student to student learning: 

  Create opportunities for students to share knowledge, explain concepts, and learn from each other. 

3.Helps students find study partners easily. 

4. Creates an organized academic support system. 

5. Promotes academic collaboration. 

 

 

## 7.Functional Requirements 

What should StudyConnect be able to do ? 

- Students can register and log in. 

- Students can create and update their  profiles. 

- Students can indicate courses they can help others with.

- Students can search for helpers by course.

- Students can request academic assistance. 

- Students can communicate with other students. 

- Administrators can manage user accounts. 


## 8. Non-Functional Requirements

How well should StudyConnect perform ?

- Security

- Reliability

- Usability

- Compatibility

- Privacy

 

 

## 9.MINIMUM VIABLE PRODUCT (MVP) 

  The first version of StudyConnect will focus on helping Babcock University students find academic assistance from other students who have knowledge of courses or topics. 

The MVP will include the following essential features: 

Student Registration: 

     Students will be able to create accounts on the platform. 

Student Login: 

    Registered students will be able to log into their accounts. 

Student profile: 

   Students will be able to provide basic information such as their name, department, level, and the courses they need assistance with, or they can assist with. 

Assistance request: 

    Students will be able to submit requests for academic help. 

Find Academic Helpers:  

   Students will be able to find other students who are willing to provide assistance. 

Communication: 

    Students must be able to communicate with one another about academic assistance. 


## 10. CONSTRAINTS 
The development and operation of StudyConnect may be affected by the following limitations:
1. Internet Access:
  Students will need an internet connection to access the platform.

2. User Participation:
  The effectiveness of the platform depends on studentsbeing willing to offer and receive academic assistance.

3. Availability of helpers:
   Students offering academic assistance may not always be available when requested.

4. Time Limitations:
    Students may have busy academic schedules.


 

 

## 11.Features excluded from the first version: 

     The initial version will not include video conferencing, online payments, AI-powered recommendations, or advance tutoring management. These may be considered for future versions.  

 

## 12.THE BASIC IDEA 

Imagine a Babcock student struggling with a Computer Science course. 

- They register on StudyConnect 

- They create their profile.  

- They search for the course they need help with. 

- They find another student who understands that course. 

- They contact that student for assistance.
